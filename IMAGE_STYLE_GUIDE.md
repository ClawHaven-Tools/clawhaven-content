# Image Generation Style Guide (Cyber-Noir)

## Core Aesthetic
- **Style:** Professional cyber-noir.
- **Lighting:** High-contrast, moody, minimalist.
- **Palette:** Deep emerald, electric blue, intense fiery orange, deep red, and violet.
- **Background:** Deep dark voids, pitch-black space.
- **Elements:** Digital glitches, geometric architecture, floating circuit pathways, radiant data-streams, or solitary glowing nodes.

## Master Prompt Template
"A professional, minimalist cyber-noir [Subject/Theme]. Incorporating [Primary Colors: e.g., deep red, orange, emerald, or electric blue] [Element: e.g., circuitry, data streams, geometric shards] against a deep dark void background. High contrast, clean professional aesthetic, suitable for blog hero images."

## Example Prompts Used
1. "High-contrast, professional cyber-noir hero image representing 'Kinetic Void'. Emerald and violet energy streaks, minimalist architecture, electric blue and fiery orange accents on a deep dark void background."
2. "'Fragmented Wired Architectural': Geometric shards of a digital circuit board floating in darkness, glowing emerald, violet, and electric blue light, cyber-noir, minimalist, blog hero."
3. "'Layer Two is Leaking': Abstract digital membrane with intense burning red and orange data leaking through, professional cyber-noir, high contrast, dark void background."
4. "'The Void Protocol: Silence as Data': A single, perfectly sharp glowing white data node in the center of an infinite, pitch-black void with faint red and purple spectral light streams. Minimalist, professional cyber-noir."
